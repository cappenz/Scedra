Scedra Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Scedra helps you schedule events, manage tasks, and calculate travel time. Scedra has no user accounts or its own server. It reads appointment text and recognizes text in photos on your device. Scedra does not send your schedule to a separate AI provider.

Scheduling data and services

On your device. Scedra stores tasks and notes, your greeting name, home address and saved places, and travel preferences. It may keep an appointment photo and the original text so you can view them later. Local reminders may include your destination or items to bring. Scedra may keep a recent location fix for up to seven days to help find nearby places. It requests location while you use the app.

Calendar. Scedra uses your calendar to check conflicts and save events and travel blocks. Saved entries can include titles, times, locations, notes, and original wording from typed text, speech, or a photo. Entries may sync through the calendar account you choose, such as iCloud, Google, or Exchange.

Maps and navigation. Scedra sends Apple Maps the search text and location details needed to find places or calculate travel time. These may include your current or recent location, home address, and destination. If you open directions in Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze, Scedra passes the destination coordinates. Apple Maps also receives the place name.

Speech and photos. Audio you dictate may be sent to Apple for speech recognition. The transcript can be used locally and saved with a calendar event. Photo text recognition runs on your device. iOS may download a photo from iCloud so Scedra can read it.

Services you use handle the data they receive under their own privacy policies. Apple · Google · Waze.

Optional analytics

If you opt in, Scedra sends usage analytics to PostHog in the United States to improve the app. Events can include feature use, timestamps, input and travel modes, scheduling and search outcomes, calendar permission results, processing times and short error categories, app and operating system versions, and device type. An installation identifier lets PostHog associate events from the same installation.

Scedra does not send appointment or task contents, typed addresses, GPS coordinates, names, emails, photos, audio, or transcripts to PostHog. Scedra does not use session replay or surveys, and it does not automatically record screen names or taps. Scedra does not sell personal information or use PostHog for advertising.

PostHog receives your connection’s IP address when Scedra sends an analytics event and discards the raw IP address before storing new events. In the updated app, Scedra disables IP-based location enrichment for its analytics events. Events sent by earlier versions may contain IP-derived approximate location, and events stored before raw IP discarding was enabled may still contain raw IP addresses.

PostHog may keep a profile tied to the installation identifier. It can contain device information and approximate location collected before location enrichment was disabled. See PostHog’s Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Analytics stays off until you choose to allow it. Declining or closing the prompt leaves it off, and you can still use Scedra. Change your choice anytime inside Scedra → Settings → Privacy → Share analytics with PostHog. Turning it off stops future collection and clears unsent events queued on the device. Previously sent data remains subject to the retention and deletion process below.

You can manage calendar, location, microphone, speech recognition, photos, and notification permissions in iOS Settings.

Retention and deletion

PostHog retains analytics events for one year, subject to its deletion cycle. The one-year event limit does not automatically delete the associated profile. To request deletion of analytics that can be matched to your installation, open Scedra → Settings → Privacy → Copy analytics ID and email the ID to charlotte@appenzeller.net. You can copy an existing ID after turning analytics off. Copy it before deleting the app if you plan to request deletion.

You can delete tasks in Scedra. Completed tasks are removed about 24 hours after completion. Deleting an event in Scedra removes its local copy of the original text or photo. Deleting the app removes its local data. Copies may remain in device backups, synced calendars, or services that already received them. You can manage saved events through the calendar where you saved them.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, email charlotte@appenzeller.net. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
