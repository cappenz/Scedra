Scedra Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

Scedra is a scheduling and productivity app that helps users create calendar events, organize tasks, calculate travel time, and plan their day.

Information Scedra Accesses

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, Scedra may access:

  • Calendar information to view, create, and edit events.
  • Location information to calculate travel times and determine when you should leave for an event.
  • Photos and screenshots that you choose to provide so Scedra can identify appointment and scheduling information.
  • Microphone and speech recognition when you choose to create an event using your voice.
  • Contacts when you choose to use contact information to help complete event details or locations.
  • Tasks and scheduling information that you enter into the app.

How Information Is Used

Scedra uses this information to provide its scheduling features, including creating and editing calendar events, organizing tasks, calculating travel time, detecting scheduling conflicts, and preparing navigation options.

Third-Party Services

Scedra may interact with services you choose to use, including Apple Calendar, Apple Maps, Google Maps, and Waze. Information handled by those services is subject to their respective privacy policies.

Data Sharing

Scedra does not sell users' personal information.

Permissions

Scedra requests permission before accessing protected information such as your calendar, location, microphone, photos, or contacts. You can change these permissions at any time through the iOS Settings app. Some features may not work if the required permission is disabled.

Data Retention

Calendar events saved through Scedra are stored in your Apple Calendar and remain there until they are edited or deleted. Other information is retained only as necessary to provide Scedra's features.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by Scedra. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Scedra changes. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
